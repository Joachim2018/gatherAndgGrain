OUR STORY / 02
The beginningEst. 2018

A neighbourhood restaurant with mud on its boots.

Gather & Grain started with a borrowed grill, a handful of local growers, and a simple question: what if dinner felt like being invited in?

Today, the answer lives in every market box that arrives at our door, every loaf that comes out of the oven, and every table that gets a little louder as the night goes on.
